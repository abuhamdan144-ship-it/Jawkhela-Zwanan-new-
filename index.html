Who we are

A village square for the young generation

Founded in 2019 by twelve friends with one football and a borrowed classroom, Zwanan Jawkhela (“Jawkhela Youth”) is now the beating heart of village life — run by youth, for everyone.

  • ✓Youth-led, elder-guided. A 9-member youth council decides, with a council of elders advising.
  • ✓Open to all. Boys and girls, students and workers — membership is free, forever.
  • ✓Visible impact. Every project publishes its budget and results on the village board.
